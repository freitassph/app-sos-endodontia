## Selection decision

The first favicon candidate was rejected because its horizontal loop and central bulb were too complex at 16–32px and too close to a generic medical staff symbol. The second candidate was selected because it keeps a compact shield/canal silhouette and remains legible at 32px.

Create ONE ultra-simple proprietary symbol only, matching a premium Swiss clinical Open Graph identity: a compact deep-midnight-navy shield outline containing one bold mineral-emerald endodontic canal curve that narrows toward the bottom.
